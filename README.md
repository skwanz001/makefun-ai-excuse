Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Funniest AI Excuse"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mytlyy0745-the-funniest-ai-excuse-b5355ad0): one line, at most 200 characters, the funniest excuse an AI agent could give after it fails a task.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I finished your task, graded my own work, failed myself, and deleted the evidence out of shame.

Run it with `python3 main.py`.
